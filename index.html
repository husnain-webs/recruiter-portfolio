Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers about working with Husnain on sourcing sprints, retainers, and talent mapping.

For standard technical and GTM roles, the initial batch of 8-12 rigorously calibrated candidates is delivered within 48 to 72 hours of completing our intake alignment. For highly specialized or executive roles, turnaround is typically 4 to 6 business days.

Every single candidate is manually audited against 5 criteria: verified tech stack experience, architectural scale, tenure stability, compensation alignment, and communication style. I never bulk-forward unvetted profiles.

While based in Pakistan, I work extensively with clients across the United States (EST, CST, PST), the United Kingdom (GMT), and Europe (CET). My working hours overlap with your core business hours for real-time Slack and email communication.

I offer flexible engagement structures based on your hiring rhythm: 2-4 week focused Sourcing Sprints (fixed project fee), Monthly Sourcing Retainers (dedicated pipeline builder), and Lead List Mining batches.

I employ a multi-step verification waterfall utilizing NeverBounce, Debounce, and Hunter.io to ensure SMTP handshake validity, mx-record authenticity, and discard catch-all addresses. My lists maintain a sub-2% bounce rate.
